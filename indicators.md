# Indicator Reference

## TB case detection rate

**Formula:** notified TB cases / estimated incident TB cases × 100

Use the official program estimate of incident TB cases as the denominator. This indicator can exceed 100% when notifications include backlog, referral cases, population-estimate errors, or differences between modeled incidence and routine notification data.

## Malaria incidence per 1,000 population

**Formula:** confirmed malaria cases / population × 1,000

Confirm whether the reporting standard uses confirmed cases, suspected cases, or all reported malaria cases.

## HIV treatment coverage

**Formula:** people living with HIV receiving ART / estimated people living with HIV × 100

The numerator and denominator should refer to the same geographic area, age group, and reporting period.

## HIV incidence percentage

**Formula:** new HIV infections / population at risk × 100

Routine program data may not directly measure HIV incidence. In official reporting, incidence may instead come from surveillance estimates or epidemiological models.
